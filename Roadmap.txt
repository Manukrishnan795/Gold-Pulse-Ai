Product Name: GoldPulse AI

Tagline: Know what moved Gold before you trade.

🎯 MVP Goal

Build a simple website where a gold trader opens one page every morning and gets:

“Everything important that happened overnight and could affect Gold today — summarized in 3–5 minutes.”

🚀 7-Day GoldPulse AI MVP Plan
Day 1 — Define the product

Build only these sections:

Inflation

Don't build accounts, payments, mobile apps, alerts, etc. yet.

Day 2 — Build the AI pipeline

Use:

News Sources → Backend → Claude API → Database → Website

Claude should process each article and return structured information:

Title
Source
Published time
Summary
Gold relevance: 0–100
Impact: Bullish / Bearish / Neutral
Importance: High / Medium / Low
Affected factors
Why it matters

Then Claude generates the final:

Gold Daily Briefing

Day 5 — Test with real traders

Give it to 5–10 people who actually trade Gold.

Don't ask:

"Do you like my website?"

Ask:

Every morning:

"Did this save you time?"

"What information was missing?"

"Which section did you actually read?"

"Would you check this before trading?"

"Would you pay AED 30/month for this?"

These answers are more important than your own assumptions.

Day 6 — Improve the AI

🔥 Most Important MVP Feature

Don't make the website:

"AI summarizes Gold news."

Make it:

"AI tells me what actually matters for Gold today."

That's the differentiation.

For every news item:

What happened?
Why does it matter to Gold?
What Gold driver does it affect?
How important is it?
What should I watch next?

That is what traders are actually paying for.

The goal should be:

First usable Gold briefing within 2–3 days.

Don't wait until the whole SaaS is perfect.

Build → give to traders → observe → improve → charge.

If 10 Gold traders start opening GoldPulse every morning without you reminding them, then you have something worth turning into a serious SaaS.
